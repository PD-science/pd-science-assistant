# Paul et al. 2026 STEM-PD hESC dopaminergic cells knowledge note

## Citation

- Title: Human embryonic stem cell-derived dopaminergic cells for Parkinson’s disease: a phase 1/2 open-label trial
- Journal: Nature Medicine
- Year: 2026
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04525-0
- Trial registration: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05635409; EudraCT 2021-001366-38; EU trial no. 2024-511237-35-00
- Article type: phase 1/2, open-label, multicenter, first-in-human, single-arm, dose-escalation trial

## Intervention

- Product: STEM-PD, human embryonic stem cell-derived dopaminergic progenitor cells from clinical-grade hES cell line RC17.
- Delivery: bilateral intraputaminal transplantation.
- Low dose: 3.54 x 10^6 cells per putamen; intended yield about 100,000 surviving dopaminergic neurons per putamen.
- High dose: 7.08 x 10^6 cells per putamen; intended yield about 200,000 surviving dopaminergic neurons per putamen.
- Surgical delivery: five tracts per putamen; four deposits per tract for low dose, eight deposits per tract for high dose.
- Immunosuppression: 12 months triple regimen, tapered months 12-15; included methylprednisolone, basiliximab, tacrolimus, azathioprine, and prednisolone.

## Participants

- N=8 enrolled, all from TransEuro observational cohort.
- Sites: Skåne University Hospital and Cambridge University Hospital; surgeries at Skåne University Hospital.
- Cohorts: low dose n=4, high dose n=4.
- Seven participants completed 12-month follow-up; one high-dose participant died 10 weeks after transplantation.
- Baseline median age: 63 years, range 58-70.
- Median disease duration: 14.5 years, range 12-18.
- Six participants male.
- All had Hoehn-Yahr stage 2 in OFF state at baseline.

## Primary Endpoint

- Number and nature of adverse events and serious adverse events within 12 months.
- Absence of space-occupying lesions on cranial MRI within 12 months.

## Key Safety Findings

- Three SAEs:
  - transient worsening of PD symptoms and anxiety after surgery, resolved.
  - visual hallucinations after medication adjustment and urinary tract infection, resolved.
  - fatal disseminated pulmonary aspergillosis with CNS involvement during active immunosuppression.
- No SAE attributed to the cell product or surgical device.
- No MRI evidence of tumor formation or abnormal tissue growth at 12 months.
- No graft-induced OFF dyskinesias observed.
- 373 non-serious AEs: 194 low dose, 179 high dose.
- Immunosuppression-attributed AEs: 33% low dose, 56% high dose.

## Graft Survival and Imaging

- F-DOPA PET at screening, 6 months, and 12 months.
- Low-dose putamen/caudate F-DOPA ratio at 12 months versus baseline:
  - left +3.2%
  - right +5.7%
- High-dose putamen/caudate F-DOPA ratio:
  - left +14.9%
  - right +11.5%
- Participant 6 high dose: left putamen F-DOPA uptake +22.5%; putamen/caudate ratio +24.9%.
- Right putamen/caudate ratio significant in combined analysis (P=0.016); left not significant (P=0.109).
- FE-PE2i dopamine transporter PET showed no significant differences at 12 months.

## Clinical Outcomes at 12 Months

- MDS-UPDRS III OFF improved in 3/4 low-dose participants and 1/3 high-dose completers.
- Low-dose median MDS-UPDRS III OFF change: -3.0 points.
- High-dose median MDS-UPDRS III OFF change: +9.0 points.
- Six of seven completers reduced dopaminergic medication.
- LEDD reduction versus baseline: 15.6% low dose, 27.5% high dose.
- High-dose ON-time without troublesome dyskinesia: 12.8 h to 15 h.
- High-dose OFF time: 2.9 h to 1.4 h.

## Interpretation

- Early 12-month data support feasibility and short-term safety of STEM-PD transplantation.
- Main risk signal is associated with immunosuppression, especially opportunistic infection risk.
- Imaging suggests graft survival in at least some deposits, more clearly in high-dose participants.
- Clinical efficacy remains exploratory and uncertain due to small sample size, open-label single-arm design, no sham or randomized control, and short follow-up relative to graft maturation.
- No participant had normalization of putaminal dopamine PET signal or discontinued dopaminergic medication.
- Longer 36-month follow-up is needed for durability, graft maturation, and functional benefit.

## Limitations

- N=8 only; seven completed 12 months.
- Open-label, single-arm, nonrandomized.
- Primary endpoint safety, not efficacy.
- Medication changes can confound motor outcomes.
- PET putamen/caudate ratio can overestimate graft effects because caudate degeneration continues.
- Requires immunosuppression; optimal regimen remains uncertain.
