All patients had undergone DBS surgery for idiopathic Parkinson’s disease between April 2010 and April 2016 and received two quadripolar DBS elec- trodes (model 3389; Medtronic). Before surgery, patients received preoperative MRI and neuropsychological testing to exclude structural or psychiatric comorbidities. During sur- gery, microelectrode recordings were performed to verify lead placement. Clinical variables, including age, sex, L-DOPA re- sponse and L-DOPA equivalent dose (LEDD) at baseline were recorded. Postoperative improvement scores were recorded 12 months after surgery under medication OFF and DBS on/off conditions using clinical DBS stimulation parameters.
